by-product of a mutable Git log.

## Contribution credits

Future durable releases should credit people according to the work they
perform, including:

- maintainers;
- code contributors;
- data and archival contributors;
- validation and quality contributors; and
- civic, financial, and subject-matter advisors.

Contributors should add or update an entry when their work becomes part of a
release. Modified versions should retain the historical creator credit, record
their own maintainers and contributors, and distinguish their changes from the
original project.

For a machine-readable citation, see [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff). For the
project's origin and licensing notice, see [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md).
