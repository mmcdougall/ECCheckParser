### Historical sequence

1. **October 2019 screening:** The January 2020 letter reports that the State
   Auditor's financial screening ranked El Cerrito seventh most fiscally
   challenged, citing reserves, liquidity, and pension costs (letter, PDF p. 1).
2. **January 13, 2020 request:** The letter seeks legislative approval for an
   audit. It describes depleted reserves, short-term borrowing, rising pension
   obligations, and the absence of an effective long-term financial response
   (PDF pp. 1-2). This request precedes the completed audit.
3. **March 16, 2021 findings:** The audit identifies El Cerrito as high risk and
   reports overspending, weak budget development and monitoring, and an
   inadequate recovery plan (PDF pp. 3 and 5; appendix B, printed p. 37, explains
   the program and the February 2020 approval to audit).
4. **December 19, 2024 removal:** The follow-up removes El Cerrito's high-risk
   designation after corrective action (PDF p. 3 and printed pp. 5-9). It rates
   four risk areas fully addressed and ongoing-cost reductions partially
   addressed. Removal does not mean every financial concern disappeared.

The State Auditor's financial-risk dashboard screening and formal high-risk
audit designation are different steps. These records document their dates;
they do not establish any new risk designation after the 2024 follow-up.
