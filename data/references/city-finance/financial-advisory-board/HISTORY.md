# Financial Advisory Board: origins and purpose

Historical note prepared November 17, 2026. This is an editorial summary, not
an official City history.

## Establishment and legislative history

The City established FAB through **Ordinance 2000-3**, codified as **El Cerrito
Municipal Code section 2.04.300**, to bring residents into City financial
deliberations. The City's November 20, 2012 staff report identifies citizen
input as the founding purpose and traces later amendments: **2007-2** revised
member appointments, and **2010-1** clarified responsibilities. The 2012
proposal removed dissolved Redevelopment Agency references and added review of
Council compensation. The published code's history records adoption of
**2012-03 on December 18, 2012**.

Sources: [2012 agenda bill](2012-11-20-fab-enabling-legislation-amendment-agenda-bill.pdf),
PDF pp. 1 and 3-4;
[published enabling code](2026-11-17-ecmc-2.04.300-financial-advisory-board.pdf),
PDF p. 1, especially the ordinance-history lines at the bottom.

## Why an advisory board?

A March 19, 2010 Institute for Local Government case study describes FAB as a
forum for residents' financial concerns and an additional review of proposed
budgets, fees, staffing, financial policies, audits, and debt. It reports that
staff valued the board's ability to anticipate questions, improve financial
documents, and discuss ideas publicly. Its account leaves final policy
authority with the Council and describes the board's work as improving public
understanding and making Council budget hearings more efficient.

Source: [Institute for Local Government, "City of El Cerrito - Budget Advisory
Committees Story"](https://www.ca-ilg.org/case-story/city-el-cerrito-budget-advisory-committees-story).
This paragraph preserves a summary, not a complete copy of that third-party
article.

The saved code assigns the five-resident board budget and long-term-plan
review, monitoring of revenues and expenditures, financial and investment
policy review, requested financial evaluations, audit review, debt review,
and recommendations to improve financial reporting. These continuing duties
provide the legal context for the board's advisory role.

Source: [ECMC 2.04.300](2026-11-17-ecmc-2.04.300-financial-advisory-board.txt),
paragraph A and duties 1-7. The
[City's FAB page](https://www.elcerrito.gov/105/Financial-Advisory-Board)
links the saved PDF as its enabling legislation.

## Scope of the evidence

FAB's creation in 2000 predates the 2019-2021 State Auditor intervention.
The collected sources do not support describing that later crisis as the
reason FAB was established.

The saved enabling law is the City's published codified section with its
amendment history. The original signed 2000 ordinance and its contemporaneous
Council deliberations are not collected here. These sources establish the
year, ordinance number, and stated purpose; they do not establish a more
specific original political or financial trigger. The 2012 agenda-bill
attachment is explicitly a proposed ordinance, with an incomplete number and
unsigned approval block.
