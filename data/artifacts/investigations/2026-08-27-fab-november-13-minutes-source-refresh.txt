Recorded November 13 outcomes

1. Comprehensive Financial Policy review

Motion/Second: Motion to table the Review of the Comprehensive Financial Policy
to the December meeting by Member White, second by Vice-Chair McDougall.

Vote/result: Ayes: Chair Carvel, Vice-Chair McDougall, Members Szlatenvyi and
White. Noes: None. Absent: Youth Member Wattson-Lamprey Singer. Motion Passed.

Scope treatment: This is a passed procedural deferral under the workplan's
Comprehensive Financial Policy objective. It does not change the August 26
text report's count of successful non-routine actions, which excluded the
January 27, 2026 procedural deferral on the same basis.

2. FAB calendar for 2026

Motion/Second: Motion to adopt the calendar with one edit moving the June
meeting from June 23 to June 4 by Member White, second by Member Szlatnvyi.

Vote/result: Ayes: Chair Carvel, Vice-Chair McDougall, Members Szlatenvyi and
White. Noes: None. Absent: Youth Member Wattson-Lamprey Singer. Motion Passed.

Scope treatment: This is a routine scheduling action outside the workplan-rooted
motion report.

3. Church-property demolition recommendation

Motion/Second: By Vice-Chair McDougall to recommend to Council that the City
moves forward with the demolition of the Church property, second by Member
Szlatnvyi.

Vote/result: Ayes: Vice-Chair McDougall, Members Szlatenvyi. Noes: Member
White. Abstained: Chair Carvel. Absent: Youth Member Wattson-Lamprey Singer.
Motion Failed.

Scope treatment: This failed General Fund fund-balance motion does not belong
in the successful-motion text report. Its relationship to the workplan's
long-term financial-plan/reserves objective must be decided before it is added
to a regenerated full workplan ledger.

Artifact status

The detailed August 25 JSON and HTML reports remain dated snapshots and must
not be treated as current full-outcome ledgers: their source map and aggregate
outcome counts predate this recovery. Regenerate them together from an approved
curated ledger before replacing their historical conclusions.
