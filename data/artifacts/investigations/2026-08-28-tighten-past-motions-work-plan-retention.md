## Actual purpose — avoid double counting

This work began as a crosswalk, not as a stand-alone accomplishments report.
Its purpose is to compare the **proposed current FAB work plan** against the
actions already claimed in the FY 2025/2026 accomplishments material, so the
same accomplishment is not presented again as a new work-plan item.

For each proposed work-plan item, the eventual comparison should classify it
as one of the following:

- **new:** no prior accomplishments claim covers it;
- **carry-forward:** prior work supports it, but the new item has a distinct,
  unfinished next phase; or
- **already claimed:** omit it as a new item, or explicitly identify the
  narrower remaining work rather than restating the past accomplishment.

The motion list below is an evidence inventory for that comparison; it does
not itself determine whether a proposed work-plan item is duplicated. The
local archive has an August 25, 2026 agenda for an accomplishments/work-plan
item but no corresponding packet or minutes, so it does not yet provide the
proposed new work plan needed to finish this crosswalk.
