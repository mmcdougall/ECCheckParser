EL CERRITO FINANCIAL ADVISORY BOARD
REFRESHED 2026-08-30
SUCCESSFUL NON-ROUTINE MOTIONS ROOTED IN THE FY 2025/2026–2026/2027 WORKPLAN

Scope

The Financial Advisory Board adopted the workplan on January 28, 2025, with four ayes and no noes. City Council later recorded the workplan as “Received and Filed” on March 4, 2025. This search covers the available FAB minutes from that adoption through the June 4, 2026 meeting.

Scope sources: FY 2025/2026–2026/2027 Workplan, p. 17; January 28 FAB minutes in the February 25, 2025 FAB Agenda Packet, p. 19; March 4 City Council minutes in the April 15, 2025 City Council Agenda Packet, p. 8.

Seventeen successful non-routine actions are retained below: six directly tied to a named workplan objective and eleven materially related fiscal or service-delivery actions. “Direct” and “related” are analytical labels, not completion findings. An action is included when the minutes say that it was approved or passed, or when they record an affirmative vote. Routine minute approvals, officer elections or appointments, failed or no-second motions, and the January 27, 2026 procedural deferral are excluded. FAB action does not establish City Council action or implementation.

2. June 24, 2025 — Section 115 investment evaluation

Motion/Second: To make a recommendation to the Council regarding the Section 115 Investment selection. Motion by Michael McDougall/Second by Janos Szlatenyi.

Recorded recommendation: With the recent Council adopted resolution to contribute and addition $1.5 million to the Section 115 the Financial Advisory Board (FAB) has reviewed the Section 115 investment plan and available investment portfolio options. The FAB would like to make a recommendation to stay in the current investment portfolio, Moderately Conservative, knowing that the City expects to use the funds in the next several years to help smooth out the City’s CalPERS contributions.

Vote/result: Ayes: Vice-Chair Carvel; Members Michael McDougal, Janos Szlatenyi. Noes: None. Absent: Chair Kimberly White; Youth Member Clive Watson-Lamprey Singer. Motion Approved.

Source: June 24 minutes in the July 22, 2025 FAB Agenda Packet, CivicClerk file 2762, p. 2.

11. January 27, 2026 — ACFR review and expenditure control

Motion: Motion by Member White to submit recommendation to the Council regarding the FABs review of the ACFR, second by Member Szlatenvyi.

Recorded recommendation: FAB reviewed the ACFR at our 1/27 meeting. We want to recognize the City is in a different financial position than it has been in the past (pre-2020). We want to acknowledge the work of the Finance department in putting in place sound budget practices. However, we still have work to do. Many of the increases in revenue are due to circumstances outside of our control (e.g. ARPA, RPTT). We want the city to focus more on the items it can control, specifically expenses, which have increased over time. We recommend particular attention be paid to controlling and reducing expenses.

Vote/result: Ayes: Chair Carvel, Vice-Chair McDougall, Members White and Szlatenyi, Youth Member Watson-Lamprey Singer. Noes: None. Absent: None. Motion passes.

Source: January 27 minutes in the February 12, 2026 FAB Agenda Packet, CivicClerk file 3175, p. 15.
